# Surface: portfolio home (single page)

Scope: the whole one-page site, `index.html` + `src/`. Mode: **Experience**: the
work leads from the first viewport.

Audience: engineer interviewer arriving from an internship application (primary),
recruiter skimming (secondary). Action: open a project, repo or the CV; email
Arya. Proof: resume figures and repo facts in PRODUCT.md / reference/profile.md.
Constraints: BRIEF.md D1–D18. User ruled out: too dark or edgy; slow to reach
projects.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Swiss objectivity as the argument: every big claim set on the grid is
followed by its measured proof. Refuses the dark dev-portfolio of chips, cards
and neon, and refuses ECHO's black-first mood.

OWN-WORLD: Light offset-paper sheet (#f1f1ec), ink black (#0c0c0c), one grey,
and one pool-tile blue (#1d5bd6) used ONLY for evidence: proof marks, measured
scales, links to proof. A visible 12-column grid with hairline column guides and
register ticks. Heavy neo-grotesk caps at poster scale against tiny 11–13px
labels; nothing in between. Black-and-white objective imagery. Square corners,
1px rules, no shadows, no gradients, no glass. Lane-line rules (the swim motif)
divide sections.

STORY: The visitor reads a claim ("I build AI that shows its work"), watches its
evidence land, then sees four projects, each a claim with its own proof. They
learn where Arya has been and how Arya works, and leave with the CV or an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The light sheet with the grid showing. Top bar: "ARYA PATHARE"
left; Work / About / Experience / Contact; CV button right. A three-line
headline in heavy caps spans columns 1–10 at ~12vw cap scale: "TOOLS THAT /
SHOW THEIR / WORK." (amended during build from "AI THAT / SHOWS ITS / WORK.":
proof 2, the Internship Finder, has no AI in it, so the AI wording would make
one of its own footnotes false; PRODUCT.md principle 2). Each line, once set,
gets a numbered blue proof mark (1 2 3) linked to a footnote row under the
headline naming a real mechanism: every recommendation cites a source
(Investment Agent); every rejected posting logged with its rule, ~11,000
postings (Internship Finder); every change previewed before it happens (Excel
Voice). A small
"Arya Pathare, CS at UNC Chapel Hill, seeking Summer 2027 internships" block
sits at columns 1–4 bottom. The placeholder silhouette stands at columns 9–12
behind the third line. Project 01 peeks at the bottom edge.

FORM: Neue Grafik Evidence Poster, candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list
(footnoted paper, heat sheet, Swiss poster, code review, wall labels, worksheet,
exhibit tags). Seed key eed221b1. Raises: measured scales under every figure
(oscilloscope); evidence lands last and hard (dossier); oversized index numerals
and a horizontal experience axis (racing); a real shader caustic behind ABOUT ME
(shader portal); literal section labels (streetwear).

Signature interaction: claim → proof. Type sets with scroll-velocity motion
blur; the blue proof mark lands last with a short hard ease. Pinned scenes: the
ABOUT ME plate grows to fill the viewport over a live caustic; experience
scrolls horizontally; projects pin one by one with their proof column.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
